Method

The IR & Aerochrome turn

With my camera I propose to see not just documentary fixation of visible signs — but an exploration of energy, stories, and reality that human eyes are simply not built to perceive.

I use full-spectrum converted cameras with 590nm, 650nm, 720nm, 850nm, and IR-Chrome filters to obtain the final chromatic register directly in-camera, without post-hoc staging or synthetic color manipulation.
